## Section 2: Resume Translation

Employers outside healthcare don't always know what a "med-surg ratio" or a "code blue" means in business terms. Translate your experience into outcomes they recognize: accuracy, deadlines, people, and systems.

| Nursing-speak (what you did) | Plain English / resume language | Example bullet you can adapt |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Managed a med-surg floor, 6:1 patient ratio | Prioritized competing workloads across multiple simultaneous accounts | "Prioritized and safely managed caseloads of up to 6 concurrent accounts; maintained 100% documentation accuracy during 12-hour shifts." |
| Administered medications and IV therapies | Executed regulated protocols with precision under strict compliance rules | "Administered precise, protocol-governed treatments with zero documentation errors across 500+ patient encounters." |
| EHR charting and documentation | Maintained compliant records in electronic systems of record | "Maintained accurate, audit-ready records in electronic health record system serving a 30-bed unit." |
| Code blue / rapid response | Crisis escalation and incident response under tight deadlines | "Led emergency escalations during codes; coordinated multi-person response under pressure to stabilize critical situations." |
| Preceptor to new grads and float staff | Onboarded, trained, and mentored new team members | "Precepted 8 new hires; developed onboarding checklists that cut ramp-up time by an estimated 20%." |
| Patient and family advocacy | Stakeholder management, communication, and conflict resolution | "Advocated for patients and families; resolved concerns by translating complex information into plain-language action plans." |
| Care coordination across departments | Cross-functional coordination and vendor/stakeholder liaison | "Coordinated discharge plans across physicians, pharmacy, PT, and social work to reduce delays and improve outcomes." |
| Infection control and safety compliance | Compliance auditing and risk-mitigation procedures | "Implemented infection-control protocols; audited unit compliance and retrained staff on safety standards." |

### Quick resume tips
- Drop medical jargon. Say "accounts," "projects," "systems," "stakeholders," and "outcomes" instead of unit-specific terms.
- Lead with results, not duties: "reduced," "trained," "coordinated," "maintained," "resolved."
- Add one numbers line where you can (patients per shift, staff trained, years of experience, error-free record rate).
- Keep a "master resume" with everything; copy-paste the relevant pieces for each application.
